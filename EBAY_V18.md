# v0.18 eBay search improvements

- All product searches default to Buy It Now, sorted by Price + Shipping: lowest first (`_sop=15`).
- Searches use a quoted product name and exclude frequent accessories, empty boxes, proxies, and single cards. These exclusions are heuristic and can hide some legitimate listings.
- Original deluxe/Mythos products remain individually searchable under their campaign, with an additional complete-collection eBay search at campaign level.
- eBay searches remain **unverified**. Search links are not proof of condition, completeness, or availability.
